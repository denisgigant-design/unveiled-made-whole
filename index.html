    Notice the particular action or experience being highlighted.

One point for each correct answer. No timer, no pressure. At the end, you can practise any moments you missed.

A simple Bible study.

Made Whole explores body, soul and spirit using a Christian teaching model, anchored in 1 Thessalonians 5:23. Body refers to physical life; soul highlights thoughts, emotions and will; spirit highlights spiritual life and relationship with God.

The Bible sometimes uses “soul” and “spirit” in overlapping ways, and Christians understand these distinctions differently. The game’s labels are learning aids. They are not a claim that every verse gives a rigid definition.

Scripture quotations are from the King James Version. Explanations and everyday examples are study commentary, not quotations.
